# Application for expedited review: SAMA pilot (template for the SVNIT IEC)

**Title.** Pilot study of camera-based heart-rate measurement across skin tones (SAMA pilot).
**Principal investigator.** Dr Sudhakar Mishra, Department of Artificial Intelligence, SVNIT Surat.
**Category requested.** Minimal risk; expedited review (ICMR National Ethical Guidelines 2017,
section 4.8: research involving no more than minimal risk, non-invasive recording of healthy adults).

**Background and aim.** Remote photoplethysmography estimates heart rate from colour changes in
facial video. Its error is known to be larger for darker skin, in dim light and in compressed video.
This pilot tests the recording set-up, synchronisation and analysis for a planned 120-participant
study (submitted to ANRF PM-ECRG), and estimates the variability needed for its sample size.

**Design.** Single 25–30 minute laboratory session per participant; no intervention, no drug, no
invasive procedure.

**Participants.** 12–20 healthy adult volunteers (18–60 years), SVNIT students and staff, of every
skin tone. Exclusions: pacemaker or implanted cardiac device; known atrial fibrillation or other
sustained arrhythmia. No vulnerable groups; students are recruited by open notice, never by their
own teachers, and declining has no academic consequence.

**Procedures.** Screening questions; Monk Skin Tone rating by researchers and self-rating; a
fingertip pulse oximeter; video of the face with a webcam (and optionally a phone) during rest, a
mock teleconsultation (ten everyday health questions), and serial subtraction (2 minutes), under a
desk lamp and under room light. Total recording about 18 minutes.

**Risks and their management.** Minimal. Mild, transient stress from serial subtraction: brief,
participant may stop at any time, debriefing. Discomfort at being filmed: explained in advance,
right to stop and to withdraw data. Privacy of face video: see data protection.

**Benefits.** None directly; societal benefit of fairer camera-based health measurement.

**Consent.** Written, informed, tiered (video used only within SVNIT, or shared under a data-use
agreement). Information sheet and consent form attached (forms 01 and 02).

**Data protection.** Participant codes only; identifying details only on paper consent forms in a
locked cabinet. Video on an encrypted offline PC and encrypted backup, never on a cloud service;
access limited to the study team and logged. Derived numeric data (no faces, no names) may be
published openly. Compliant with the DPDP Act 2023 and its 2025 Rules. Retention ____ years.

**No clinical use.** No reading is returned to participants as health information; the software is
a research tool, not a medical device.

**Compensation.** ______________ (or none).

**Timeline.** Recording in November 2026 after approval; analysis within two weeks.

**Attachments.** Protocol (pilot/PROTOCOL.md), information sheet, consent form, CRF, Monk rating
sheet, recruitment notice.
