  If ethics approval does not arrive in time, build Figure 6 from Track A only, title section 5
  "Preliminary analysis of public data", and state that the 10 protocol pilot sessions are
  scheduled for months 2–3 (already in the plan). A public-data figure is weaker than your
  own data but far stronger than no figure.

  What the figure must show to persuade a reviewer: that your pipeline runs end to end
  (feasibility), that error moves with ITA° even in a small sample (panel A), and that the
  compression ladder works and changes error (panel B). It does not need significance.


  Pilot protocol and how to make Figure 6
  Mini-protocol (25 minutes per volunteer). Five minutes of set-up, then under light 1 (desk
  LED, about 500 lux): rest 3 minutes, mock consultation 3 minutes, mental arithmetic 2
  minutes. Switch to light 2 (room light, about 100 lux) and repeat. Keep a colour chart in
  view at the start of each half. Flash an LED (or clap) at the start and end of each block for
  synchronisation.

  Equipment you need now: a USB webcam that can record uncompressed video, any
  Android phone on a stand, a Polar H10 with a logging app that saves RR intervals (for
  example Polar Sensor Logger), a 24-patch colour chart, a desk LED lamp and a lux-meter
  app. A colorimeter is better for ITA°; if you have none yet, use ita_from_patch.py on
  calibrated frames and call the values approximate.

  Analysis, step by step.

   1. Cut every recording into 30-second windows using the flash marks.
   2. Reference heart rate for each window: 60,000 divided by the mean RR interval (ms)
      from the Polar H10.
   3. Make the codec ladder from the uncompressed webcam video:
      ./transcode_ladder.sh webcam.avi ladder_P01 .
   4. Run POS, CHROM and pretrained TS-CAN from rPPG-Toolbox on the lossless video and
      on each H.264 level, and save heart rate per window.
   5. Get each volunteer's ITA° (colorimeter, or python ita_from_patch.py cheek.png --
        white white_patch.png ).
   6. Put all rows into one CSV (columns below) and run python pilot_figure.py
        pilot_hr.csv --method POS .
   7. Read off the numbers for the brackets in section 5. From the same CSV, compute the
      between-person SD of MAE and the within-person SD of the 500 kbps penalty, put
      them into power.py, and re-run it. If power drops below 0.8, say so in section 6 and use
      the safeguard (up to 50 per stratum).
