# Datasheet — virtual SAMA-120 (SAMA-Sim cohort, seed 7)

- **What it is:** 120 simulated participants (40 per ITA° stratum, 20 F / 20 M), generated by
  `sim/samasim.py` (`make cohort`). SYNTHETIC. No person is represented; no face exists.
- **Composition:** ITA° uniform within lighter (28–50), middle (10–28), darker (−25–10); ages 18–60;
  resting HR, ln RMSSD, reactivity, perfusion, beard and spectacles drawn per participant
  (`make_participant`). The uniform ITA° ranges are the proposal's; Indian data suggest a darker
  and wider distribution (I-18).
- **Recording design:** 8 seated blocks × 2 lights at the proposal's durations (D-13); devices:
  reference camera (60 fps lossless; 720p30 ladder source), webcam, phone A (auto-exposure), phone
  B (adaptive); codec 500/250 kbps (surrogate v0 in this cohort, D-23); ECG beats with artefact bursts.
- **What is stored:** patch-level features per 30 s window, baseline estimates, HRV summaries
  (`results/cache/`, not committed: regenerate with `make cohort`).
- **Intended use:** testing the analysis design and the software. **Not** for estimating any
  property of real people.
- **Known gaps:** no rolling shutter, no real face geometry or specular highlights, codec as an
  analytic surrogate, PTT model simple, motion statistics invented (sensitivity: pools).
